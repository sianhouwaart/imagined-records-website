BEHIND IMAGINED RECORDS

Sebastiaan J.
Houwaart.

FOUNDER & CREATIVE DIRECTOR · IMAGINED RECORDS

Imagined Records was founded by Sebastiaan J. Houwaart, the creator behind the label and its fictional artists. It began with a simple idea: use imagination and new creative tools to make original music with stories and emotion at its heart.

Sebastiaan shapes the label's creative direction, developing artist identities, stories and musical ideas, and guiding the release process. AI is part of the production process; the creative decisions are human.

From Oliver W.'s reflective indie folk to VAYNE's darker sound, each artist explores a different world. They share the same ambition: to create music that means something to the person listening.
