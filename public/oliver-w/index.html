Some songs are about the moments that change everything. Oliver W. writes about the ones that almost go unnoticed.

Oliver W. is a virtual singer-songwriter created by Imagined Records. His creative identity draws on the everyday textures of western Pennsylvania: ordinary Tuesdays, old family homes, relationships that didn't work out, and evenings you wish could last a little longer.

Warm acoustic arrangements meet alternative folk-rock energy and intimate piano performances. Honest storytelling, a little roughness around the edges, and a voice that knows when to hold back.
