04 / OUR STORY

Imagined Records develops original, fictional music artists and the worlds around them. Our artists are virtual. The creative direction is human. Artificial intelligence is part of how we bring these ideas to life.

We believe transparency and artistic ambition belong together. Our aim is simple: make music people genuinely connect with.

05 / BEHIND THE LABEL

Imagined artists.
A human beginning.

Sian Houwaart — Founder & Creative Director

Imagined Records was founded by Sian Houwaart, the creator behind the label and its fictional artists. It began with a simple idea: use imagination and new creative tools to make original music with stories and emotion at its heart.

Sian shapes the label's creative direction, developing artist identities, stories and musical ideas, and guiding the release process. AI is part of the production process; the creative decisions are human.

From Oliver W.'s reflective indie folk to VAYNE's darker sound, each artist explores a different world. They share the same ambition: to create music that means something to the person listening.
