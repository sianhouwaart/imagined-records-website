Between two worlds.

Japan / Brooklyn, New York

Born in Japan and later moving to the United States, YORU finds his creative home in Brooklyn, New York. His imagined world draws on intimate clubs, independent music culture and the energy of the city after dark.

His music brings together vulnerable, melodic vocals, understated guitar textures and contemporary alternative R&B grooves. Quietly introspective and effortlessly approachable, YORU lets the songs say what words sometimes cannot.
