# Oliver W. — Visual identity (approved direction)

**Status:** Approved canonical visual reference (31 October 2026).

## The chosen portrait

The first generated portrait, saved in the Imagined Records working conversation as:

**`mijmerende_muzikant_bij_zonsondergang.png`**

This is the image the label owner approved with “Nice!!” and then confirmed as the official face of Oliver W. with “Graag!”. It is the **sole approved visual reference for Oliver W.'s appearance**.

A subsequent image (`bard_bij_zonsondergang_met_gitaar.png`) was generated accidentally and is **NOT** an approved replacement. Do not use it as a source of truth.

## Rules for future Oliver W. artwork

1. Preserve the recognizable face and overall identity shown in the **first approved portrait**.
2. Future album artwork, artist photos, press images and social imagery should use that exact portrait as the reference.
3. Do not invent a new face or subtly change his facial features without explicit review and approval.
4. The existing website landscape concept art is temporary and should only be replaced after the approved portrait is securely added to this repository.
5. Keep the fictional/AI-assisted nature of the artist transparent.

## Asset status

The original approved portrait is committed to this repository as **`public/assets/oliver-w-portrait.png`**. The artist page (`public/oliver-w/index.html`) uses that exact file. Preserve this file as the canonical reference for future visuals.
